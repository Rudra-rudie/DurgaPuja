## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the devotional warmth, grandeur, and welcoming spirit of Bengali Durga Puja (Sharodotsav) and temple (mandir) administration. Rooted in autumnal Bengali nostalgia—Kash phool, the rhythmic resonance of the dhaak, and glowing earthen pradips—the aesthetic pairs classical sacred ornament with contemporary digital craft.

The design movement synthesizes **Festive Traditionalism with Warm Frosted Glassmorphism**:
- **Alpona & Filigree Motifs:** Geometric Bengali rice-paste art forms frames, dividers, and corner cartouches with crisp SVG rendering.
- **Diya Luminescence:** Warm saffron and vermillion radial glows illuminate card headers and vital calls to action, creating an atmosphere of sanctified presence.
- **Glassmorphic Shrine Layers:** Translucent parchment-like containers (`rgba(255, 251, 235, 0.82)`) with subtle golden borders sit above deep terracotta backgrounds, ensuring high functional utility for mandir scheduling, pushpanjali slots, bhog bookings, and donation ledger tracking.
- **Mobile-First WhatsApp Optimization:** Ultra-compact invitation cards structured with 9:16 mobile canvas ratios, high legibility for multilingual recipients, and instantaneous deep linking for RSVP and bhog tokens.

## Colors

The color palette captures the sensory energy of Sindoor Khela, marigold garlands, sacred brass bells, and ritual conch shells:

- **Primary (`#9E1B1B` - Royal Vermillion):** Used for focal CTAs, sacred headings, active navigation tabs, and divine accents. Paired with `#B91C1C` for illuminated hover and active states.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Saffron Gold):** Embodies the sacred flame of the pradip and marigold floral decor. Applied to secondary actions, active badges, ritual highlight banners, and border filigree highlights (`#F59E0B` for illuminated trims, `#FEF3C7` for festive glow fills).
- **Tertiary (`#7F1D1D` - Deep Terracotta Maroon):** Rooted in Bengal terracotta temple tiles of Bishnupur. Powers solemn structural containers, deep headers, and devotional card framing.
- **Neutral (`#1E1B18` - Charcoal Navy):** Replaces harsh jet black with an ink-black warmth that guarantees contrast against warm parchment backgrounds for long-form reading and ledger entries.
- **Background & Canvas:**
  - `Surface Base`: `#FFFBEB` (Warm Sand / Kash Phool Cream).
  - `Surface Muted`: `#FAFAF9` (Alpona Chalk White).
  - `Surface Glass`: `rgba(255, 251, 235, 0.85)` with a backdrop blur of `12px`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances ceremonial literary beauty with pragmatic operational clarity:

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Evokes the vintage lithographs and invitation patras of traditional Bengal. Used strictly for ceremonial headings, puja tithi dates (Maha Saptami, Ashtami, Sandhi Puja), and devotional invocation mantras.
- **Editorial & Narrative Body (Noto Serif):** Delivers literary dignity, warmth, and optimal legibility for ceremonial descriptions, cultural program line-ups, and committee welcome notes. Retains crisp glyph distinction across both Latin script and Bengali transliterations.
- **Functional Interface Elements (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Applied to transaction-critical components—bhog coupon codes, queue numbers, time-slot selection chips, donation inputs, and administrative table data—to avoid visual fatigue and guarantee rapid recognition.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a mobile-first fluid layout calibrated for smartphones, since festival cards and daily schedule links are primarily distributed over WhatsApp:

- **Canvas Constraints:** On mobile viewports (<640px), the content conforms to a centered vertical card flow with `margin: 1rem` to maximize screen real estate for ceremonial banners. On desktop viewports (≥1024px), the maximum content container is locked to `1180px` within a 12-column grid.
- **Alpona Padding Zones:** Cards featuring decorative Alpona borders require a minimum inner padding of `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) so that floral corner filigrees never collide with interior copy or CTA buttons.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Component stacks adhere strictly to an 8px base rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Ritual day transitions utilize `space-xl` alongside a centered ornate lotus motif divider.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth mimics the physical layers of a puja pandal—from the foundational red terracotta floors to backlit sanctum screens and floating brass diyas:

- **Level 1 (Static Terracotta Cards):** Flat parchment surfaces with crisp 1px borders of `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)` and a soft warm ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(127, 29, 29, 0.08);`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Ritual Panels & Modals):** Translucent glassmorphic overlay using `rgba(255, 251, 235, 0.90)` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Elevated with a golden-tinted drop shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -4px rgba(158, 27, 27, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.06);`
- **Level 3 (Diya Luminescence Glow):** Reserved for primary interactive elements, active Ashtami Pushpanjali slots, and VIP passes:
  `box-shadow: 0 0 24px 2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35), 0 4px 12px rgba(158, 27, 27, 0.25);`

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a roundedness factor of `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` default radius, `1rem` / `16px` for cards, `1.5rem` / `24px` for modals), honoring traditional Indian architectural arches and curvilinear terracotta friezes:

- **Invitation Cards:** Framed with `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`) outer corners, complemented by internal SVG Alpona borders that hug the inward perimeter curves.
- **Ritual Chips & Tokens:** Styled with pill-shaped radiuses (`9999px`) to mirror circular marigold beads and sacred chandan tilaks.
- **Inputs & Administrative Controls:** Grounded with `rounded-md` (`0.5rem`) to maintain architectural stability and high numeric scan efficiency.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (The Sacred Vermillion Button):** Rich `#9E1B1B` background, pure `#FFFBEB` serif or semi-bold sans text, `rounded-lg`, framed with an inner inset ring of `rgba(254, 243, 199, 0.3)`. Hover triggers a golden saffron halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary CTA (Golden Pradip):** Warm cream background (`#FFFBEB`) with a 1.5px border of `#D97706` and `#9E1B1B` text. Active states fill to `#FEF3C7`.
- **Festive Share Action (WhatsApp Optimized):** Emerald-tinted or Saffron Gold button adorned with the festive conch icon and direct share payload.

### Cards & Invitation Panels
- **Alpona Bordered Invitation Card:** A two-tier container. Outer layer is Terracotta Maroon (`#7F1D1D`), hosting an inner parchment canvas (`#FFFBEB`). Corner motifs display intricate four-point rice-flour Alpona patterns in `#D97706`.
- **Ritual Schedule Card:** Structured timeline displaying date, Bengali tithi, Sandhi Puja timestamps, and Priest details. Completed slots appear muted with a chandan paste tint; ongoing slots glow with a subtle saffron pulse animation.

### Chips & Badges
- **Tithi / Ritual Badges:** Compact pill chips with translucent backgrounds (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)`), `#9E1B1B` text, and fine `#D97706` borders.
- **Queue & Bhog Status:** Real-time indicator badges utilizing vibrant vermillion for "Full", saffron for "Fast Filling", and deep olive green for "Slots Open".

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs & Date Pickers:** Crisp `#FFFBEB` background, charcoal text (`#1E1B18`), bordered with 1px `#D97706` at 40% opacity. Focus state shifts the border to 2px solid `#9E1B1B` accompanied by an ambient saffron focus ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular radio dots and custom check glyphs styled after stylized lotus buds in Vermillion Red `#9E1B1B`.

### Mandir Management Additions
- **Bhog Coupon Token:** Digital tear-away card design with perforated dashed line divider, QR code for prasadam redemption, and live counter synchronization.
- **Donation (Daan) Ledger List:** Dense high-contrast rows alternate between `#FFFBEB` and `#FAFAF9`, displaying donor names, gotra, contribution tier, and instant PDF receipt download icons.
